Cost of Renting a Party Bus for a Chicago Blackhawks Game at the United Center: What to Expect

Planning to catch a Chicago Blackhawks game at the United Center with a group? This guide breaks down the estimated costs of renting a party bus, helping you budget for a fun and convenient game-day transportation experience.

Cost of Renting a Party Bus for a Chicago Blackhawks Game at the United Center: What to Expect

Renting a party bus for a Chicago Blackhawks game at the United Center typically costs between $600 and $1200 for a 4-5 hour rental, depending on bus size and demand. This often covers round-trip transportation from a pickup point in Chicagoland, allowing your group to enjoy the game without worrying about parking or traffic near the West Loop.

Average party bus rental rates for sporting events in Chicago

Most party bus rentals for a Blackhawks game are booked for a minimum of 4 to 5 hours. This duration accounts for travel time to the United Center, waiting time during the game, and the return trip. The specific rate you pay depends heavily on the bus's capacity and the day of the week.

For a smaller group heading to the United Center, a 10-14 passenger sprinter-style party bus might run $550-$850 for a four-hour rental. If your group is larger, needing a 16-22 passenger bus, expect to pay between $700-$1000 for the same duration. Larger buses, accommodating 24-30 people, generally start around $900 and can go up to $1300 or more for a four-hour block, especially for popular Saturday night games. Prices can also vary slightly if your pickup location is further out, like from Naperville or Schaumburg, compared to a pickup in the Loop.

Factors impacting the cost: bus size, rental duration, and game day demand

Several elements combine to determine the final price of your party bus rental for a Blackhawks game. Understanding these factors helps you budget effectively and potentially find a better deal.

Bus Size

The more passengers a bus can hold, the higher its base rental rate. Operators price their vehicles based on capacity and the associated operational costs.
