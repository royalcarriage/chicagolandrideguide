Cost Breakdown: Sprinter Van vs. Party Bus for a Chicago Bachelorette Party in the Suburbs

A comparative cost analysis of renting a sprinter van versus a party bus for a bachelorette party taking place in the Chicago suburbs, helping you make an informed decision.

For a bachelorette party in suburban Chicago, a Sprinter van typically costs $120-$200 per hour for 8-14 passengers, while a party bus runs $175-$300 per hour for 15-30+ guests. The choice depends on group size, desired amenities, and total travel time, with party buses generally offering more space and features at a higher per-hour rate.
