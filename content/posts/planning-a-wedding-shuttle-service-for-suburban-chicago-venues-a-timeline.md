**Direct answer:** For wedding shuttle services in suburban Chicago, booking 6 to 9 months in advance is ideal to secure preferred vehicles and rates, especially for popular venues like The Drake Oak Brook or Schaumburg Convention Center. Expect typical hourly rates for a 30-passenger bus to range from $150-$250, with potential for package deals.

## When to Book Your Shuttle Service

Planning a wedding shuttle service for suburban Chicago venues requires foresight. Aim to book your transportation provider **6 to 9 months before your wedding date**. This lead time is critical for several reasons. Popular dates, particularly in the late spring and fall wedding seasons, get booked quickly. Securing your shuttle early ensures you have access to the types of vehicles you need – whether it’s a fleet of vans for smaller groups or a large coach bus for everyone. For example, if your wedding is at the Grand Victoria Casino in Elgin, you'll want to get ahead of other couples planning events there.

## Gathering Guest Accommodation and Venue Information

Before you can get accurate quotes, you need to collect essential details. First, finalize your guest accommodations. If guests are staying at multiple hotels, like the Westin Lombard or the Hyatt Regency Schaumburg, list each one. Next, confirm the exact address and any specific drop-off instructions for your ceremony and reception venues. This includes knowing if your venue is at The Morton Arboretum in Lisle, which might have different access points for buses. Having this information readily available will speed up the quoting process with transportation companies.

**Checklist:**
*   List of all hotels where guests will be staying.
*   Full addresses for ceremony and reception venues.
*   Any specific venue access instructions (e.g., service entrances, gate codes).
*   Estimated number of guests needing transportation.

## Determining Shuttle Routes and Pick-Up/Drop-Off Points

Once you have venue and accommodation information, map out your shuttle routes. Consider a few scenarios:
*   **Hotel to Venue, then Venue to Hotel:** This is the most common. A shuttle might run from a block of hotels in Naperville to a venue like Cantigny Park in Wheaton.
*   **Ceremony to Reception:** If your ceremony and reception are at different locations, like a church in Downers Grove followed by a reception at a banquet hall in Oak Brook.
*   **Late-Night Return:** Offering a shuttle back to hotels after the reception concludes is a popular amenity.

For each route, determine the most efficient pick-up and drop-off points. At hotels, identify the best spots for buses to park without obstructing traffic or guest flow. At venues, confirm where shuttles should arrive and depart. For example, at a venue like The Haue Valley in Plainfield, you'll need to know their designated bus parking area.

**Example Route Planning:**
*   **Route 1:** Hotel A (Naperville) -> Venue (Wheaton)
*   **Route 2:** Venue (Wheaton) -> Hotel A (Naperville)
*   **Route 3:** Venue (Wheaton) -> Hotel B (Lombard)
*   **Route 4:** Hotel A (Naperville) -> Hotel B (Lombard)
*   **Route 5:** Venue (Wheaton) -> Hotel A (Naperville) [Late Night]
*   **Route 6:** Venue (Wheaton) -> Hotel B (Lombard) [Late Night]

## Confirming Final Guest Counts and Logistics with the Shuttle Provider

As your wedding date approaches, typically **30-60 days out**, you'll need to confirm your final guest counts and logistics with your chosen shuttle provider. This is when you'll provide your final RSVP numbers to help them determine the exact number of vehicles needed. Discuss the timing of each pick-up and drop-off with the provider. They can help you create a detailed schedule, ensuring guests aren't waiting too long or arriving too early.

For instance, if you've booked a shuttle for your wedding at The Drake Oak Brook, and you have 100 guests needing transport from two hotels, you might need two 40-passenger buses running on a loop. You'll confirm the exact departure times from each hotel and the venue. This is also the time to finalize any special requests, such as having a specific driver or ensuring the vehicles are equipped for accessibility.

Most shuttle providers will offer either an hourly rate or a flat transfer fee. For a wedding shuttle that's making multiple trips throughout the evening, an hourly rate is usually more cost-effective. If you only need a one-way transfer, like from hotels to a venue before the ceremony, a flat fee might be better. Always ask for a detailed breakdown of costs, including any potential surcharges for mileage, tolls, or extended hours.
