A private shuttle bus for a suburban Chicago wedding typically costs between $600 and $1,500 for a 4-6 hour rental, depending on the vehicle's size and the booking date. Shorter, single transfers might start around $350-$500, while larger buses for longer events or multiple routes can exceed $2,000. These figures represent common ranges for popular wedding transport options in the Chicagoland area.

## Average hourly and per-trip costs for private shuttle buses in suburban Chicago.

Most private shuttle bus rentals for weddings in the Chicago suburbs are priced hourly, with a required minimum number of hours. This minimum commonly ranges from 3 to 6 hours, especially on weekends. Per-trip or transfer rates are less common for wedding day logistics unless it's a very simple, single A-to-B route.

Here are typical hourly rate ranges for different vehicle types in the suburban Chicago market:

*   **Mini-Bus (14-25 passengers):** These are often the most popular choice for wedding guest shuttles or bridal party transport. Expect to pay $100-$150 per hour, usually with a 3-4 hour minimum. For example, moving guests from a hotel in Lisle to a venue in Naperville.
*   **Party Bus (14-30 passengers):** Offering more amenities like wrap-around seating and entertainment systems, party buses are popular for bridal parties. Rates typically run $120-$200 per hour, with a 4-6 hour minimum.
*   **Mid-Size Coach (30-40 passengers):** For larger guest counts or slightly longer distances within the suburbs, these coaches run $130-$180 per hour, often with a 4-5 hour minimum.
*   **Full-Size Motorcoach (50-56 passengers):** If you're moving a significant number of guests, perhaps from downtown Chicago to a suburban venue in Geneva or St. Charles, a full-size coach is necessary. These command $160-$250 per hour, typically with a 5-6 hour minimum.
