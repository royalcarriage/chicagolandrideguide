**Direct answer:** Limo costs in Chicago typically range from $100-$200 per hour for sedans and SUVs, and $150-$300+ per hour for stretch limos and party buses. Point-to-point transfers for airport runs can range from $75-$150 for a sedan to O'Hare or Midway, while longer distances or premium vehicles will increase these flat rates significantly.

## Hourly vs. Transfer Pricing

When booking limo service in Chicagoland, you'll generally encounter two pricing structures: hourly and point-to-point (flat rate). Hourly pricing is common for events where your needs are flexible or extend over a significant period, like a wedding day or a multi-stop night out. Point-to-point pricing is ideal for straightforward trips with a single pickup and drop-off, such as airport transfers.

Understanding which suits your needs best saves money and avoids surprises. For instance, a quick trip from your Schaumburg home to O'Hare might be cheaper as a flat rate, while a wedding spanning multiple venues and photo stops throughout the day is almost always more cost-effective hourly.

| Pricing Structure   | Best For                                                              | Typical Use Cases                                           |
| :------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hourly**          | Flexible schedules, multiple stops, extended events, waiting time     | Weddings, proms, nights out with multiple destinations      |
| **Point-to-Point**  | Direct trips, fixed pickup/drop-off, airport runs                     | Airport transfers (O'Hare, Midway), one-way events          |

## Sedan, SUV, Stretch: Realistic Ranges

The type of vehicle you choose significantly impacts the price. Basic sedans are the most economical, followed by larger SUVs, and then stretch limos or party buses, which command higher hourly rates due to their size and amenities.

*   **Sedans:** Expect to pay around $100-$175 per hour for a standard sedan. These are great for airport runs or discreet transport for 2-3 passengers.
*   **SUVs:** For slightly more space and comfort, SUVs typically run $125-$200 per hour. They can accommodate 4-6 passengers and offer a more commanding presence.
*   **Stretch Limos:** These classic choices usually start at $150-$250 per hour, with some larger or more opulent models going higher. They are ideal for groups of 6-10 passengers looking for a celebratory feel.
*   **Party Buses/Vans:** For larger groups (10-20+ passengers), party buses are the way to go. Hourly rates for these can start around $175-$300 and go up significantly based on amenities like sound systems, lighting, and bar areas.

Consider the number of passengers and the overall vibe you're aiming for when selecting your vehicle. A short trip from the Loop to a West Loop restaurant might be fine in a sedan, but a group heading to a concert at the United Center might prefer the space of an SUV or the party atmosphere of a stretch.

## Wedding-Day Example Math

Weddings are a prime example of where hourly pricing shines, but it requires careful planning to manage costs. Let's break down a hypothetical wedding day for a couple in the Chicagoland suburbs.

**Scenario:** A wedding with a ceremony in Naperville, photos in a downtown Chicago park, and reception back in Naperville.

*   **Pickup:** Bride and bridal party at Naperville hotel (9:00 AM).
*   **Ceremony:** Naperville (11:00 AM).
*   **Travel to Chicago:** Naperville to Millennium Park (11:30 AM - 12:30 PM, accounting for traffic on I-88/I-290).
*   **Photo Session:** Millennium Park (12:30 PM - 2:30 PM - limo waits).
*   **Travel back to Naperville:** Millennium Park to Naperville reception venue (2:30 PM - 4:00 PM, depending on traffic on I-55/I-355).
*   **Waiting at Venue:** Guests arrive, bridal party mingles (4:00 PM - 5:00 PM).
*   **Reception Start:** (5:00 PM).

**Vehicle Choice:** A stretch limo for the bridal party.

**Estimated Time:**
*   Pickup to arrival at reception venue: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM is 8 hours.
*   Add buffer for potential delays (traffic on the Kennedy, unexpected photo ops): Let's add 1 hour buffer.
*   Total Billable Hours: 9 hours.

**Cost Calculation:**
*   Stretch Limo Hourly Rate: $200/hour (typical for a nice stretch).
*   Total Limo Cost: 9 hours * $200/hour = $1,800.

This doesn't include gratuity, which is often customary. For larger weddings needing transport for the wedding party and guests, you might book multiple vehicles (e.g., a shuttle bus for guests and a stretch for the bridal party), each charged hourly.

## When Hourly Wins

Hourly pricing is the clear winner for several types of events and scenarios:

*   **Multi-Stop Tours:** If you plan to visit several bars in the city, hit multiple wedding venues, or do a wine tour in a region like the Fox Valley, an hourly rate allows your driver to wait while you enjoy each stop. A point-to-point rate would charge you for each individual leg, which quickly becomes more expensive.
*   **Extended Events:** For proms that start with pre-event photos and end with late-night rides home, or for a full day of wedding festivities, hourly is the only practical option.
*   **Unpredictable Schedules:** If your itinerary has a lot of flexibility or potential for delays (e.g., attending a Cubs game with uncertain game length, or a business conference with sessions running over), hourly gives you peace of mind without incurring per-trip charges for every little adjustment.
*   **Airport Runs with Waiting:** While point-to-point is good for a direct airport run, if you have a group that needs to collect luggage or you're meeting someone who might be delayed, an hourly rate might be more economical than paying for extended waiting time on a flat rate.

Consider a night out in the city. You might start with dinner in River North, then head to a show in the Loop, and finish with drinks in Lincoln Park. A stretch limo booked hourly for 5 hours at $225/hour would cost $1,125. If you tried to book point-to-point for each leg, even with conservative estimates, you'd likely exceed that amount.

## Deposit and Cancellation Norms

Most Chicago limo companies require a deposit to secure your booking, especially for high-demand dates like Saturdays or holidays. This deposit is typically 20-50% of the total estimated cost. It serves as a commitment from both parties.

Cancellation policies vary, but a common timeframe is 72 hours to 14 days prior to the service date for a full refund of the deposit. Cancelling with less notice might result in forfeiture of the deposit or a partial refund. For very large bookings or special vehicles (like a specific party bus), the cancellation window might be longer, perhaps 30 days. Always clarify the exact deposit amount and cancellation policy in writing when you book.

Failure to adhere to cancellation terms can lead to unexpected charges. For example, cancelling a wedding limo booking two days before the event might mean you still owe the full quoted price.
